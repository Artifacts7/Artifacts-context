# Designing an Agentic State

Lecture for *Designing Democracy* (KAFP 4215, Sciences Po), Session 10 "Designing an Agentic State", 20 November 2026. The opening segment, before guest Simone Parazzoli: what happens when citizens send AI agents to deal with the state, and what that breaks for governments.

## Files

- `slides.html`: the deck as presented in class, 51 slides. Open it in a browser. Self-contained: fonts and images are embedded.
- `speaker-notes.md`: the notes for every slide, in order.
- `outline.md`: the consolidated lecture outline, section by section, with sources and preparation notes.

## Structure

1. Agents have started taking actions for us
2. Citizens are sending agents to the state
3. What this means for governments

Then three closing statements hand over to Simone Parazzoli: The Agentic State, ending on "So, what does it take to make a state that is ready for AI agents?"

`slides.html` is the export from the Claude Slides artifact downloaded on the morning of the class. The deck was edited afterwards (for example, the wording of the "increase capacity" road); this file keeps the version that was shown. The part on equipping government for the agentic era was not developed beyond those three statements.
