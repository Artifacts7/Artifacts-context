# A gift from builders and makers

**Subtitle:** A few sentences, literally.
**Date:** 2024-12-27

**Status:** Published — captured from live article on 2026-10-24

**Every day this year, I set out to read something interesting**—or at least remarkable—in pursuit of a simple goal: to stay curious and, hopefully, interesting.

Most of what I read had to do with technology, **but not always.**

**The common thread, I’d say, was people who built something.** An artifact, a process, a company, a government—you name it.

And every day, **I jotted down a sentence that resonated with me.**

I kept them all in a small Moleskine notebook I carried everywhere.

By now, that’s about 366 quotes.

I went back through them, picked a handful of favorites, **and turned them into a short digital book for all the subscribers of Artifacts.**
