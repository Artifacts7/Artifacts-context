One of the reasons Artifacts exists is to examine organizations building tech products for citizens, not just consumers—technology that enables better democracy and participation.

An interview with **Josef Lentsch**, CEO and Founder of the **Political Tech Summit**, held January 23–24, 2026 in Berlin.

## The Interview

**What exactly is "political tech"?**
In democracies, governments and citizens each have distinct technological ecosystems. GovTech digitizes public administration; CivicTech empowers citizens. Political tech occupies the space between them—software, data, and tools used by political parties, unions, associations, and NGOs operating in the political sphere.

**What sparked creating the Political Tech Summit?**
Working with political organizations revealed technology's potential to enhance their work, yet "too many relied on in-house tools that weren't up to the task." The summit creates a marketplace where people and solutions connect, improving outcomes for politics and citizens.

**Who should attend?**
The primary audience is political professionals—staff, operatives, executives, department heads, and directors. Citizens are welcome too. Participants come from 40+ countries.

**What tech solutions are available?**
CRMs for activist and constituent data, content moderation, data analysis, political process monitoring, and more.

**What are this year's key themes?**

- **Power:** Campaign tech stacks, rapid-response content, field operations—voter data integration, message testing, targeting, and volunteer workflows.
- **Protection:** Institutional and campaign safety—trust and safety, incident response, threat intelligence, counter-disinformation, deepfake detection, and narrative monitoring.
- **Participation:** Broadening voices—youth engagement, AI-assisted citizen dialogues, digital assemblies, inclusive design.

**If someone wants traditional policy discussion, what won't they find?**
Sessions cap at 45 minutes—no endless debates. Instead, new practitioners focused on how things actually work.
