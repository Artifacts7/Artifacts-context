Artifacts attended the Italian Tech Week in Turin—a surprisingly balanced event with the right mix of participants spanning startups, VCs, Big Tech, institutions, and media. Genuinely international, with attendees from multiple countries.

## Key Insights from the Conference

**AI as Basic Infrastructure**
Laura Modiano, Head of Startups EMEA at OpenAI, emphasized that artificial intelligence is becoming foundational infrastructure comparable to electricity or cloud computing. European startups like Granola are building directly on top of AI rather than simply incorporating it into existing products.

**AI's Dual Nature**
While AI is the newest technology wave, it's simultaneously reshaping its predecessors. Built on cloud infrastructure, data systems, and semiconductors, AI is fundamentally changing how these foundational technologies function. Users can now interact with databases conversationally through AI interfaces.

**The Engineer's Enduring Relevance**
Engineering expertise remains essential. Rather than replacement, the focus shifts toward embedding AI intelligently within existing systems to solve real customer and citizen problems. Forward-deployed engineers will drive this integration.

**Italy's Startup Challenges**
Italian startups face critical limitations: their addressable market is too small, relying primarily on a domestic ecosystem. International talent doesn't migrate to build companies there, and funding sources remain regionally concentrated.

**The European Advantage**
Companies thinking European from inception—in product development, team composition, and capital sourcing—move faster. Navigating 27 markets and 24 languages presents complexity, but mastering this challenge trains entrepreneurs for global expansion.

**Institutional Innovation Leadership**
A recurring theme emphasized that public institutions still lag in driving genuine innovation. Tech-savvy individuals in government positions accelerate ecosystem development more effectively than traditional approaches.

**Bureaucratic Barriers**
Research by FutureProofSociety revealed that over 80% of businesses have lost growth opportunities due to excessive regulation. One-third spend more than €30,000 annually on compliance costs. Entrepreneurs dedicate approximately seven hours weekly to bureaucratic tasks.
