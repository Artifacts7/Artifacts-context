# To better understand
**Subtitle:** How and why access to data of platforms matters for us all, not just for researchers
**Date:** Nov 18, 2025
**URL:** https://artifactstech.substack.com/p/to-better-understand

---

The Mozilla Festival in Barcelona centered on a singular theme: **data access and control**. Conversations consistently returned to ensuring equitable data access, transparency, and ownership—particularly crucial as AI systems increasingly depend on data people barely control.

## Key Initiatives

**Mozilla's Data Collective** emerged as a significant platform for responsible data exchange in AI training. Its director stated that "data is power and that power should belong to the people and organizations who are creating that data."

The Wikimedia Foundation presented "The Wikipedia Test," where participants designed policies compensating Wikipedia for AI company scraping—illustrating how data extraction affects content creators.

## The Core Issue

Data sharing practices represent a fundamental challenge to internet health. Without visibility into platform mechanics, power concentrates with corporations and states rather than citizens and researchers.

## Knight Georgetown Framework

The framework addresses two dimensions:
- **What data** is critical for access (priority categories)
- **How** to establish efficient access mechanisms

While regulations like the Digital Services Act mandate researcher access, platforms continue resisting implementation.

## Featured Interview: Brandi Geurkink
*Executive Director, Coalition for Independent Technology Research*

**On Data Access Importance:** Independent researchers provide essential counterbalance to corporate incentives. Data access "levels the playing field and opens a window where companies have intentionally created a black box."

**Critical Research Areas:** Under the DSA, companies must develop harm mitigations. Evaluating their effectiveness requires empirical data. Understanding viral content distribution—particularly identifying paid amplification—strengthens media ecosystems.

**Current Barriers:** Over 60% of independent researchers face significant obstacles:
- Platforms eliminated useful research tools (CrowdTangle shutdown, X's $42,000/month API pricing)
- Legal intimidation including cease-and-desists and account suspensions
- Excessive gatekeeping despite regulatory requirements

**Real-World Impact:** Research into TikTok's role in the Romanian election demonstrated how algorithmic amplification affects electoral integrity.

**Regulatory Assessment:** The DSA helps but requires refinement. Clear compliance standards, consistent timelines, and minimal verification barriers would better serve the law's purpose.

**Dream Project:** Investigating Google's monopoly power through independent economic research, particularly given ongoing antitrust discovery.
