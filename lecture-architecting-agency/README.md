# Architecting Agency

Lecture for *Outthinking the Algorithm: Managing in the Age of AI* (Sciences Po), November 2026. How to manage AI agents that act on our behalf, and why the answer is mostly about context.

## Files

- `slides.html`: the deck, 54 slides. Open it in a browser. Arrow keys or click to move, `N` shows speaker notes, `F` goes fullscreen. Fonts load from Google Fonts; images are in `assets/`.
- `speaker-notes.md`: the notes for every slide, in order.
- `outline.md`: the lecture outline (v2), with timings, purposes and sources per section.
- `brief.md`: the original brief and first draft of the lecture.

## Structure

1. What is an agent?
2. Computers that use computers
3. Why agents need to be managed
4. Are agents outlaws?
5. Working with agents: writing things down
6. Architecting agency through context
7. Case study: my second brain
8. Case study: Artifacts
9. Safeguards and limits

Then an after-class exercise and Q&A.

`slides.html` is exported from the Claude slides artifact the deck was built in. The two animations (the agent loop and the context-loading example) play in the browser.
