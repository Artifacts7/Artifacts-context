Why AI Labs Ask to Be Regulated

A theoretical scaffold for reading the labs as actors in an advocacy field — not firms reacting to policy, but norm entrepreneurs authoring it. Three pillars build the WHY; a fourth bridges to the HOW.

THE QUESTION

Why do AI labs advocate for regulation of their own industry — and what narrative do they use to do it?
