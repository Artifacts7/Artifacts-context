## Verbatim quoted passages

Reported by Camila Domonoske, NPR ("Elon Musk Warns Governors: Artificial Intelligence Poses 'Existential Risk'," NPR, July 17, 2017):

- On the threat: "AI is a fundamental existential risk for human civilization, and I don't think people fully appreciate that." NPR reports he called AI "the scariest problem."

- On job displacement, in response to Gov. Sandoval: NPR reports that Musk said "robots will do everything better than us."

- On proactive regulation: "I think by the time we are reactive in AI regulation, it's too late." NPR notes he called regulation generally "not fun" and "irksome," but argued that in the case of AI the risks are too high to allow it to develop unfettered.

- On sounding the alarm: "I think people should be really concerned about it. ... I keep sounding the alarm bell."

- On the first regulatory step — government gaining "insight" into the actual status of current research: "Once there is awareness, people will be extremely afraid. As they should be."

- NPR also reports Musk warned governors that artificial intelligence poses a "fundamental risk to the existence of human civilization" and an "existential threat" to human civilization.
