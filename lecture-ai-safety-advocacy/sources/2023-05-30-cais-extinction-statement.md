Statement on AI Risk (verbatim, the full statement):

"Mitigating the risk of extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal-scale risks such as pandemics and nuclear war."

CAIS notes that AI experts, journalists, policymakers, and the public are increasingly discussing a broad spectrum of important and urgent risks from AI, but that it can be difficult to voice concerns about advanced AI's most severe risks. The succinct statement is intended to overcome that obstacle, open up discussion, and create common knowledge of the growing number of experts and public figures who take these severe risks seriously.
